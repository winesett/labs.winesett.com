Quest 3 mixed-reality study

Edge of Nothing

A floor-level hatch that suggests impossible depth without moving the real room. Start closed, seated, and a few steps back.

Hatch closed · 3.4 m ahead · compact

Passthrough is requested when supported. If it is unavailable, this study uses a dim dark-room VR fallback.

The luminous ring is a real-floor reference, not a surface scan. The hatch is always positioned ahead of you and cannot move under your body.

Right trigger: open / close · B: emergency close · stick up/down: size · stick left/right: distance · A: depth style · left Y: reset.

Seated mode recommended. Do not walk toward the hatch, step onto it, or use it near stairs, edges, pets, or other people. Stop immediately if depth cues feel uncomfortable.
