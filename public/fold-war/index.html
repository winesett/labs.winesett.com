Your next move is closer
than you think.

  1. 01

    Pick a game.

    Choose Naval Duel or tic-tac-toe. Tap Vs computer to play offline on one device.

  2. 02

    Or bring a friend.

    For nearby play, open the same game on both devices. One player hosts; the other selects that host under Nearby games and enters the six-digit code. Keep Wi-Fi on and allow Local Network access.

  3. 03

    Play it your way.

    Choose Phone or Tabletop layout on each device. Take turns. Call the rematch.

Solo play needs no network connection. Nearby multiplayer needs two devices, each with Fold War open. Phone and Tabletop layouts are selected manually.
