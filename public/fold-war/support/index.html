Fold War support

Have a question, found a bug, or want to share an idea? We’d love to hear it.

Talk to Winesett Labs.

support@winesett.com ↗

Put “Fold War” in the subject. For a bug, include your device, operating system, app version, and what happened. A screenshot helps—please leave out passwords and other private information.

A few quick answers.

Where can I download Fold War?

Fold War is in development and is not available for public download yet. The Fold War homepage will link to the official release when it’s available. The help below describes the current development build.

How do we start a game?

Each player needs Fold War on their own device. Choose the same game. One player taps Host game and shares the six-digit code; the other taps Join game and enters it. Keep Wi-Fi on and allow Local Network access when asked.

Why can’t I find or join my friend?

Keep both devices nearby with Fold War open, and check that you selected the same game. Try connecting both devices to the same Wi-Fi network. Guest or public networks may block connections between devices.

On iPhone or iPad, check Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network and enable Fold War. Then leave the waiting room, host again, and use the new code. Apple’s Local Network guide ↗

What’s the difference between Phone and Tabletop?

Phone is the compact layout. Tabletop separates the play areas across the screen. Choose either layout in the app on each device. Switching is manual; the current app does not detect a device’s hinge angle.

The folded device in our brand artwork is a concept. Supported devices will be listed with the public release.

What happens if I leave or switch apps?

In the current build, leaving the game or putting the app in the background ends your connection. Keep the app open during a match. If disconnected, host and join a new game; the previous match is not restored.

How do we play again?

After the match, both players choose Play Again to start a rematch. To choose a different game, leave the match and select the same new game on both devices.

Do I need an account?

No. The current app has no accounts, ads, or analytics SDKs. Nearby devices exchange game information directly. Read the privacy policy for details about the app, website, and support email.
