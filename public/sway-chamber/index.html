Sway Chamber

A fixed room and gently drifting wall pattern can make a still body feel as if it is moving. The camera never moves; the effect is only a visual illusion.

StoppedGentle · Lateral drift

Right trigger: start/stop · B or Y: emergency stop · right stick up/down: intensity · left/right: mode.
Start seated or with a real-world support. Stop immediately if you feel unsteady, nauseated, or disoriented. This app does not measure physical sway.
