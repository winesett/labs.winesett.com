Orikata press kit

Orikata is an origami app from Winesett Labs. It guides you through making real paper objects, then keeps the finished creations on a personal shelf.

Available now on the App Store · Five free lessons. Version 1.0.1. Availability varies by storefront while the international rollout completes.

The app

Choose a pattern and follow each fold on your phone while making it with real paper. Replay a movement when needed, then save the completed creation to your shelf.

Orikata includes puppy, heart, tulip blossom, boat, and traditional crane lessons. The crane is an intermediate project. Progress and creations stay on the device.
