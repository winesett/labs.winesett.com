Orikata press kit

Orikata is an origami app from Winesett Labs. It guides you through making real paper objects, then keeps the finished creations on a personal shelf.

Development preview · Not publicly available yet. The planned launch includes five free lessons. Screenshots show the app in development.

Download the press kit ↓Press enquiries ↗

In one sentence.

Orikata helps you fold real paper with clear interactive lessons, a choice of paper colors, and a personal shelf of completed creations.

A little more.

Orikata makes room for a small, hands-on creative break. Choose a pattern, put a sheet of paper beside your phone, and follow each step at your own pace. Watch the movement, compare your real paper, and tap Done when you’re ready to continue.

The planned first collection includes a puppy, heart, tulip blossom, boat, and traditional crane. Lessons are free, with no ads, accounts, or subscription. Progress and creations stay on the device. Ordinary iPhones use touch demonstrations; an additional interaction for compatible folding hardware is in development.
