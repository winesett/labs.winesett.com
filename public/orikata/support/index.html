Help with the current Orikata preview. The app is still in development; public downloads are not available yet.

Talk to Winesett Labs.

support@winesett.com ↗

Put “Orikata” in the subject. For a problem, include your device, OS version, app version, pattern, and step. Describe what your paper looks like and what you expected. Only include screenshots or photos you’re comfortable sharing.

Start with the right sheet.

Open My shelf, choose Create new, and select a pattern. Read its materials before starting: puppy, heart, and tulip use square paper; the boat uses a rectangle. Choose a screen color that helps you compare the two sides of your real paper.

Keep the paper beside your phone. Turn and slide the paper to match the screen; don’t put it inside a folding device.

Watch, compare, then confirm.

On an ordinary phone, use the step’s Show control to watch the movement. Make that fold with your real paper, compare the result, then tap Done. Watch again repeats the demonstration. Help opens fuller instructions.

On compatible folding hardware, ordinary hinge-aligned folds can follow the phone’s closing movement. Compare the result on the outer display, tap Done, then open fully to continue. Some operations, including turnovers and selective-layer steps, use an open-device demonstration instead.

If you reopen without confirming, the current fold remains unconfirmed. A demonstration alone never counts as completing the real fold.

Recover from a confusing step.

Use Help to reread the instruction and Watch fold or Watch again to repeat the movement. Check the starting side, marked corner, and existing creases. If Redo previous step is available, first restore your real paper to the shown earlier shape, then follow the step again.

Starting a new sheet replaces the unfinished lesson after confirmation. Keep folding cancels that choice. Completed creations stay on the shelf.

Leave and come back.

Use the back control to return to My shelf. Continue folding resumes the saved lesson. The app stores confirmed steps, paper color, and completed creations on this device; it does not provide an account or developer-run cloud sync.

If a save cannot be opened, Orikata keeps it untouched and offers retry or restoration when a previous valid copy is available. If saving fails, follow the on-screen message, make room on the device if needed, and try saving again. Contact support before deleting the app if you want to preserve the collection.

Your shelf and sharing.

Finish the lesson to add a creation to My shelf. Open a creation to rename it, prepare a keepsake image for the system share sheet, or delete it after confirmation. Cancelling a share does not send anything.

Photo import is not part of the launch experience. Photos saved by earlier development builds are preserved during upgrades and removed with their associated creation when you deliberately delete it.

Availability and privacy.

The planned launch includes puppy, heart, tulip blossom, boat, and traditional crane. All are free, with no ads, account, or subscription. Final device and OS requirements will appear on the App Store listing when it becomes available.

Read the privacy policy for details about local saves, optional sharing, this website, and support email.
