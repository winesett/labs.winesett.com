Your privacy.

Orikata keeps your lessons and collection on your device. The app has no accounts, advertising, tracking, or analytics SDKs.

Effective October 25, 2026 · Current development version

Who we are.

Orikata is made by Cody Winesett, operating as Winesett Labs. This policy covers the current app, its pages at labs.winesett.com/orikata/, and Orikata support email. Questions or privacy requests: support@winesett.com.

When you use Orikata.

The lessons run on your device. We do not operate an Orikata server that receives your folding activity, collection, name, email address, photos, contacts, or location. The app does not contain advertising, cross-app tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs.

The app stores lesson progress, pattern versions, paper colors, creation names, completion dates, and information needed to remember deletions in its local app storage. It keeps a previous valid save for recovery and preserves an unreadable save when recovery is needed. These files remain in the app’s storage. Device backups may include app data according to your operating-system and backup settings.

Photos and sharing.

The launch experience does not import or take photos. If you used an earlier development version that saved photos, an upgrade preserves those local files. Deleting the associated creation removes its legacy photo after the collection change is saved.

If you choose to share a creation, Orikata prepares an image locally and opens the system share sheet. You choose whether and where to send it. The selected destination handles the image under its own privacy practices; Orikata does not upload it to a Winesett Labs server.

Your choices and local data.

You can rename or delete a completed creation in the app. Removing Orikata from the device removes its local app storage; copies in device backups are managed separately through your platform settings. We cannot access or delete a local collection remotely. There is no account to delete and no developer-run cloud sync.

When you contact support.

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any information or attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond and investigate the issue. Please send only what is useful for that request.

Support mail is forwarded by Cloudflare to a Gmail inbox. Those providers process messages to deliver and store email. We keep support correspondence as needed to handle your request and related follow-up. You can ask for access, correction, or deletion by contacting the address above; some information may need to be retained to meet legal obligations.

When you visit this website.

Cloudflare hosts and delivers these pages and may process technical request information such as your IP address, requested URL, browser information, and request time to provide and secure the service. The hosting service also adds a Cloudflare Web Analytics beacon to these pages to measure page visits and loading performance. This website measurement is separate from the Orikata app, which has no analytics SDK. We do not add advertising trackers, an account system, or a mailing-list form. Artwork and other page assets are served with the site.

See Cloudflare’s Web Analytics documentation, Cloudflare’s privacy policy and Google’s privacy policy for those providers’ practices.

Platform services.

Your operating system and app distribution or testing services are separate from Orikata. Apple may process platform or diagnostic information under its own settings and privacy policy. Development builds may write local diagnostic files for testing; that event-writing code is excluded from Release builds. This does not add an analytics SDK to the app.

Changes to this policy.

Orikata is in development. If its features or data practices change, we will update this page and its effective date. Any notice or permission required for a new data practice will be handled before it begins.
