Before you begin.

What do I need?

A phone and a sheet of paper. Puppy, heart, and tulip use a square sheet, around 15 × 15 cm. The planned boat lesson uses a rectangle. Each lesson lists its materials.

Do I need a folding phone?

No. Ordinary iPhones use touch controls to show each fold. An additional interaction for compatible folding hardware is in development. Keep the paper beside your phone.

Will it be free?

Yes. All five planned launch lessons will be free, without ads, accounts, or a subscription. Lessons and your collection work on your device.

When can I get it?

Orikata is still in development. There is no public download yet. We’ll add the App Store link here when it’s ready.
