FOLLOW THE LESSON

Watch the fold.

Tap Show fold to see the movement. Replay it when you need another look.

Fold your sheet.

Keep your paper beside the phone. Match the corners and crease shown on screen.

Compare, then confirm.

Tap Done when your paper matches. The lesson waits for you.
