Before your first fold

A few small details.

Do I need a folding phone?

No. On an ordinary iPhone, tap to watch each step and confirm when your paper matches. We’re also developing an interaction for compatible folding phones, where closing and opening the phone helps demonstrate suitable folds. Final compatibility will be listed with the release.

Does the phone fold the paper for me?

You make the real folds. Orikata shows the movement and the shape to aim for. Keep your paper beside the phone; it doesn’t need to touch or go inside the device.

What kind of paper should I use?

Each lesson lists its materials. The puppy, heart, and tulip use a square sheet, around 15 × 15 cm. The boat uses a rectangle. A sheet with different colors on its two sides can make the instructions easier to follow.

Is it free? Does it work offline?

The launch lessons will be free, with no ads, account, or subscription. Lessons and your collection work on your device. Opening this website or sending a support email needs an internet connection.

When can I download Orikata?

Orikata is in development and isn’t publicly available yet. We’ll add the App Store link here when you can install it. In the meantime, explore the preview and press kit or get in touch.
