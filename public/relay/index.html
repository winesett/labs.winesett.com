From your Echo to your agents

Tell Alexa who it’s for and what you need. Relay sends the request to that agent, so you can pick it up in their app.

Explore Relay

Relay is a prototype in development. Grok’s Echo connection has been tested; Muse and Dot are still taking shape. The hackathon demo uses a simulated Alexa+ interface with a live Dot mailbox. Physical Echo reply playback remains unverified. Follow the project or get in touch to learn more.
