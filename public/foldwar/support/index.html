A few quick answers.

Where can I download Fold War?

Fold War is in development and is not available for public download yet. The Fold War homepage will link to the official release when it’s available. The help below describes the current development build. Features may change before release. The first release is planned to be free and available in the United States.

Can I play on my own?

Yes. Choose Naval Duel or Tic-tac-toe, then tap Vs computer. Solo play works offline and does not need Local Network permission. In Naval Duel, choose your fleet arrangement and tap Ready to begin.

How do we start a nearby game?

Each player needs Fold War on their own device. Open the same game on both devices. One player taps Host game and shares the temporary host name and six-digit code. The other taps Join game, selects that host under Nearby games, enters the code, and taps Connect. Both selecting the host and entering the code are required. Keep Wi-Fi on and allow Local Network access when asked.

Why can’t I find or join my friend?

Keep both devices nearby with Fold War open, and check that you selected the same game. The host must still be hosting. On the joining device, tap Refresh, select the host name, and enter its current six-digit code. If the host remains missing, try the same Wi-Fi network; guest or public networks may block connections between devices.

On iPhone or iPad, check Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network and enable Fold War. After returning from Settings, start a fresh Host/Join session and use the newly displayed code. Apple’s Local Network guide ↗

What’s the difference between Phone and Tabletop?

Phone is the compact layout. Tabletop separates play areas across the screen. The current build uses Phone on compact unfolded screens and selects Tabletop when the system reports an active fold on supported iPhone Duo systems. A manual selector is available on larger screens.

Brand artwork is illustrative. Read about the iPhone Duo layout. Final compatibility will be listed with the public release.

What happens if I leave or switch apps?

In the current build, leaving a game or putting the app in the background ends the session. Keep both apps open during nearby multiplayer. To play after leaving, start a new solo game or a fresh Host/Join session; the previous match is not restored.

How do we play again?

After a solo game, tap Play again for another round. After a nearby multiplayer game, both players must tap Play again to start a rematch. To choose a different game, leave the match and select the same new game on both devices.

Is Fold War free? Are there ads or paid extras?

Yes. Both games, computer opponents, and nearby multiplayer are free to play. No ads, in-app purchases, subscriptions, or account required.

Do I need an account?

No. The current app has no accounts, ads, or analytics SDKs. Nearby devices exchange game information directly. Read the privacy policy for details about the app, website, and support email.
