FOLD WAR — Unfold game night.
From Winesett Labs
Updated October 20, 2026

STATUS
In development. No public download or announced release date yet.
The first release is planned to be free in the United States.

DESCRIPTION
Fold War brings Naval Duel and tic-tac-toe to iPhone and iPad. Play the computer
offline on one device, or challenge someone nearby on two devices. On supported
iPhone Duo systems, an active fold selects the Tabletop layout automatically.

FACTS
Creator and copyright owner: Cody Winesett
Studio: Winesett Labs
Games: Naval Duel; tic-tac-toe
Solo: offline, one device
Nearby multiplayer: two devices, Wi-Fi on, Local Network permission
Joining: select the host, enter its six-digit code
Accounts: none required
Current build minimum: iOS 17 / iPadOS 17
Automatic fold layout: supported iPhone Duo systems with iOS 27.1 or later
Compact unfolded display: Phone layout
Larger display: manual Phone / Tabletop selector when not actively folded
Initial launch plan: free; United States; no Mac or Apple Vision Pro distribution
Final App Store compatibility and availability will govern the public release.
