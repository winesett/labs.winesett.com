About Fold War

In Naval Duel, arrange five ships and take turns searching for the opposing fleet. In tic-tac-toe, make three marks in a row. Play the computer offline, or host a nearby match and share a six-digit code with the other player.

On supported iPhone Duo systems, folding selects Tabletop automatically. Compact unfolded screens use Phone layout. Read the layout and compatibility details.
