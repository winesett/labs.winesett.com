Before you download

Is Fold War available?
Not yet. The first release is planned to be free in the United States. This page will link to the App Store when it is available.
Is it really free? Are there ads?
Yes. Both games, computer opponents, and nearby multiplayer are free to play. No ads, in-app purchases, subscriptions, or account required.
Do I need an iPhone Duo?
No. The app also has a compact Phone layout. iPhone Duo adds a fold-aware Tabletop layout. Final device compatibility will be listed with the release.
Can two people play on one device?
Nearby multiplayer uses two devices, one per player. Solo play uses one device.
Does it need an account or internet connection?
No account is required. Solo play works offline. Nearby play uses local networking with Wi-Fi enabled; there is no remote matchmaking.
