Ships to find.

Win by sinking all five opposing ships. Play the computer on one device, or a nearby rival on their own.

In development. Fold War is not available for public download yet. This guide describes the current development version; details may change before release. The first release is planned for the United States. Free to play. No ads. No in-app purchases. Just game night.

Get your fleet ready.

Choose Naval Duel from Games. For a solo round, tap Vs computer. Tap Shuffle if you want a different fleet arrangement, then Ready.

In nearby play, each person uses their own device and both players must be ready before the match begins. The host takes the first shot; in solo play, you go first.

One shot at a time.

  1. Choose a target. On the Target board, select a square you haven’t tried.
  2. Confirm the shot. Tap Fire at [coordinate]. Selecting a square and firing are separate steps.
  3. Read the result. Use the hits and misses already on the board to decide where to search on your next turn.
Example turn

If B4 is untried and it’s your turn, select B4, then tap Fire at B4. A hit gives you a reason to investigate nearby squares; a miss rules that square out. This is a rules example, not a captured match.

Two boards. Your choice of layout.

The Target board records your search. Your fleet shows the ships you’re defending. In Phone layout, use Target and Your fleet to switch between them. Tabletop separates the play areas across the screen.

The current build selects Tabletop automatically when the system reports an active fold. Compact unfolded screens use Phone layout; larger screens also offer a manual selector. See the iPhone Duo layout.

Solo or across the table.

Vs computer

One device, no network connection, and no Local Network permission needed.

Nearby rival

Two devices with Fold War open, Wi-Fi enabled, and Local Network access allowed. Follow the nearby setup guide →

Ready for another round?

After a solo game, tap Play again. After a nearby match, both players tap it to start the rematch. Leaving the game or putting the app in the background ends the current session; previous games are not restored.

For a quicker board, meet tic-tac-toe. For connection questions, visit Fold War support.
