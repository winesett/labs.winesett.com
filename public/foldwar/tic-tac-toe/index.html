Marks in a line.

Make a horizontal, vertical, or diagonal line of three. A full board without a winning line is a draw.

Pick an opponent.

Choose Tic-tac-toe from Games, then Vs computer for an offline round on one device. You play X and go first.

For a match with someone nearby, open tic-tac-toe on both devices. One player hosts and the other joins. The host plays X and goes first. See the two-device setup →

Make your move.

  1. Wait for your turn. Check the turn status before choosing your next square.
  2. Tap an empty square. Your mark claims that position; occupied squares stay occupied.
  3. Look for a line. Three of your marks in a row wins. Block your opponent’s line while building your own.
Example turn

If your opponent has two marks in the top row and the third square is empty, playing there blocks that immediate line. First check whether you can complete a winning line of your own. This is a rules example, not a captured match.

One device alone. Two together.

Play offline

Vs computer needs no network connection or Local Network permission.

Play nearby

Each player uses their own device. Keep Wi-Fi enabled and allow Local Network access. There’s no account or remote matchmaking.

Choose your view.

Compact unfolded screens use Phone layout. An active fold selects Tabletop automatically on supported iPhone Duo systems; larger screens also offer a manual selector. See how the layout follows the fold.

Call the rematch.

When a solo round finishes, tap Play again. In a nearby match, both players tap it to begin again. Keep both apps open: leaving or switching apps ends the current session, and that match cannot be resumed.
