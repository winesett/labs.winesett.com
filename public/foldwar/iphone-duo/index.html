Fold War on
iPhone Duo

Two board games with a layout that follows the fold.

Fold War is an iPhone app in development by Cody Winesett at Winesett Labs. It includes Naval Duel and tic-tac-toe, with offline computer opponents and nearby matches between two devices. Free to play. No ads. No in-app purchases. Just game night. The first release is planned for the U.S.; there is no public download yet.

What changes when you fold it?

In the current development build, an active display fold selects Tabletop automatically. Naval Duel puts the target board on one side of the hinge and your fleet on the other. Tic-tac-toe separates its status area from the playing board.

A compact unfolded screen uses Phone layout. In Naval Duel, tabs let you switch between Target and Your fleet. On larger screens, the layout selector also lets you choose Phone or Tabletop manually.

Can I play alone?

Yes. Open either game and choose Vs computer. Solo play works offline on one device, without an account or Local Network permission.

How does two-player work?

Both players need the app on their own device. One hosts and shares a six-digit code. The other selects that host and enters the code. Keep Wi-Fi enabled, allow Local Network access, and keep both apps open. There is no remote matchmaking.

Looking for games to try on iPhone Duo?

Fold War is being built for quick board-game rounds: Naval Duel when you want to search and plan, tic-tac-toe when you want a shorter match. Both support offline solo play and nearby two-player play.

What will I need?

Current build minimum
iPhone with iOS 17 or later; iPad with iPadOS 17 or later.
Automatic fold layout
Supported iPhone Duo systems with iOS 27.1 or later. Simulator behavior has been checked; physical Duo testing is not claimed here.
Offline play
Choose Vs computer. One device; no account or network permission needed.
Nearby matches
Two devices running the same Fold War version, Wi-Fi enabled, and Local Network access allowed.
Availability
No public download yet. Planned free U.S. launch; final compatibility will be confirmed on the App Store.

You do not need a Duo to play. Smaller unfolded screens use the Phone layout; larger screens also offer a manual Tabletop selector.
