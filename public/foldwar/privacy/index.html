Fold War privacy policy

Effective October 20, 2026

The current Fold War app has no accounts, ads, or analytics SDKs. Solo games run on your device; nearby multiplayer exchanges game information directly between the players’ devices. We do not operate a server that receives your games.

Who we are

Fold War is made by Cody Winesett, operating as Winesett Labs. This policy covers the current Fold War app, its pages at labs.winesett.com/foldwar/, and Fold War support email.

Questions or privacy requests: support@winesett.com.

When you play

Solo play against the computer runs on your device without a network connection. Solo moves and results are not sent to Winesett Labs.

For nearby multiplayer, the app connects two nearby players using local network discovery and a six-digit joining code. It uses a temporary, nonpersonal host name to help the devices find each other. The devices exchange connection information, game choices, moves, results, and rematch messages needed to play.

This information goes between the participating devices, not to a Winesett Labs game server. We do not receive your match history or collect your name, email address, contacts, or location through the app. There is no advertising, cross-app tracking, or third-party analytics SDK in the current app.

Permission and data on your device

Local Network permission allows Fold War to find and connect to the other player. You can turn it off in your device’s settings; nearby play will then be unavailable. Solo play against the computer does not require this permission.

Your Phone or Tabletop layout preference is saved on your device. Match state is held during play; the current app does not provide a saved match history or cloud sync. You can change the layout in the app or remove the app to remove its local app data.

When you contact support

If you email us, we receive your email address, your message, and any information or attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond and investigate the issue. Please share only what is needed to explain the problem.

Support mail is forwarded by Cloudflare to a Gmail inbox. Those providers process messages to deliver and store email. We keep support correspondence as needed to handle your request and related follow-up. You can ask us to delete it by emailing the address above; we may retain information where required by law.

When you visit this website

Cloudflare hosts and delivers these pages. In doing so, it may process technical request information such as your IP address, requested URL, browser information, and request time to provide and secure the service.

We do not add advertising trackers or analytics scripts to the Fold War pages. Fonts and artwork are served with the site. There is no mailing-list signup, account, or contact form on these pages.

See Cloudflare’s privacy policy and Google’s privacy policy for their own processing practices.

Platform services

Your operating system and any app distribution or testing service you use are provided separately. Apple may process platform, purchase, or diagnostic information under its own settings and privacy policy. This does not mean Fold War includes an analytics SDK.

Your choices

You can stop local network access in device settings, remove local app data by removing the app, and choose what to include in a support email. Contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information you have sent us. We do not sell personal information or share it for targeted advertising.

Changes to this policy

Fold War is in development. If its features or data practices change, we will update this page and its effective date. Changes that require a notice or permission will be handled before the new data practice begins.
